====================================================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
====================================================================

1. What does your service do, in one line?
We set up automated renewal reminders and lead follow-up sequences for your team, built around the Excel sheets you already use.

2. Who do you work with?
Loan and insurance distribution agencies and brokers in Thane and Mumbai. Most of our clients have a team of 2 to 20 people and run everything on Excel today.

3. Do I have to stop using Excel?
No. We usually keep Excel as your master record and connect it to automatic follow-ups. If you later want to move to a proper system, we can help, but it is not required.

4. How do renewal reminders work?
Your policy or loan renewal dates from your Excel sheet feed a reminder sequence. The right person gets a nudge by WhatsApp or email before the due date, so nothing depends on someone remembering to check a sheet.

5. How do lead follow-ups work?
New leads get an automatic first response and a fixed follow-up schedule. If a lead goes quiet, the system reminds your team again instead of leaving it to chance.

6. Will my team have to learn new software?
Barely. Your team keeps working in Excel or WhatsApp for most tasks. We set things up so the follow-ups happen in the background.

7. What tools do you use?
Only mainstream, low-cost tools like Google Sheets or Excel, WhatsApp, email and a workflow automation platform. We do not lock you into expensive software, and there are no accounts you must buy before we start.

8. How long does setup take?
A simple renewal reminder setup takes 3 to 5 working days. A full lead and renewal setup takes about 2 weeks. We give you a clear timeline after the free audit.

9. What does it cost?
Setup starts at Rs 15,000 one-time, and monthly support starts at Rs 2,500. See the PRICES section below for packages.

10. Are there extra or hidden charges?
No hidden charges. You pay the setup fee once and the monthly support fee. Any optional extras, like extra users or paid software you choose, are quoted upfront and in writing.

11. Do you offer a free audit or trial?
Yes. We start with a free 30-minute Excel audit. We look at your sheets and tell you exactly what can be automated and what it will cost. You pay nothing until you approve the plan.

12. What is the 30-day money-back guarantee?
If your agreed follow-up metrics do not improve within 30 days of going live, we refund your setup fee. You keep the setup, so you lose nothing.

13. What do you need from me to get started?
A copy of the relevant Excel sheets, access to a phone number or email you want reminders sent from, and about an hour of your time for a walkthrough. We handle the rest.

14. Is my client data safe, and who owns it?
Your data stays yours. We work on a copy or a shared sheet with limited access, we do not share your data with anyone else, and we remove our access when the project ends.

15. Can it work for both loan and insurance renewals?
Yes. Loans, policies, health and motor renewals, and general insurance all work. Each product can have its own reminder timing and message.

16. Can it handle multiple branches or teams?
Yes. Reminders can go to different people based on branch, product or owner. We set the routing during setup.

17. What happens if a follow-up gets missed?
The system logs every reminder and flags missed or failed ones, so you can see what happened and act on it. We also set an alert for failures so they do not pile up silently.

18. What support do I get after setup?
Monthly support covers fixes, small changes and a check-in on your numbers. We respond within 1 business day, and urgent WhatsApp issues within 4 business hours.

19. What if I want to change or stop later?
You can change your plan or stop any time with 30 days' notice. Your sheets, data and credentials stay with you, and we hand over everything.

20. How do I get started?
Book your free 30-minute Excel audit by phone or WhatsApp. We will review your sheets, suggest the simplest setup, and share a fixed quote the same week.
